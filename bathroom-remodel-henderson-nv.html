Frequently asked questions

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

A refresh (vanity, fixtures, paint) can take a few days. A full remodel with new tile and a shower conversion usually takes a few weeks, depending on materials and scope.

Can my tub be turned into a walk-in shower?

Often, yes. Tub-to-shower conversions are one of the most requested bathroom projects in Henderson, and we can help you plan one.

Who does the plumbing and electrical?

Plumbing and electrical work is contracted directly with appropriately licensed contractors. We help line them up and coordinate scheduling so you have one point of contact.

Do you help with design choices?

Yes. We can help you choose tile, vanity, fixtures and layout so everything works together.
